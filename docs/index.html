Adaptive Water Purifier

A continuous water purifier that recirculates water through a particulate filter and activated carbon until onboard sensors indicate it meets a “clean” threshold, then opens an outlet to dispense the treated water. It also reports the water’s pH and automatically refills the treatment chamber when the level drops so purification can continue without user intervention.

Sensors: Turbidity and pH Stages: Initial fill; Continuous filtering; pH measuring; Dispense Website = official report
